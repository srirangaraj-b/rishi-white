01 / Threat Profile

The Analyst Behind The Investigations

I enjoy understanding how attacks happen, not just detecting that they did. Every alert is a story waiting to be reconstructed — and I read logs like a forensic narrative.

My day involves investigating Windows Event IDs, dissecting network traffic, mapping malware behaviour to MITRE ATT&CK techniques, and tracing attacker movement across fragmented telemetry.

I find satisfaction in reconstructing timelines from scattered evidence — correlating Sysmon process creation with authentication logs, DNS queries, and file system artifacts until the full picture emerges.

I build detections that reduce analyst fatigue. A well-tuned Sigma rule or Suricata signature saves hours of manual triage. I care about signal-to-noise ratio because a SOC that drowns in false positives misses real threats.

I improve continuously through practical labs, malware analysis, SOC simulations, and real incident investigations. The threat landscape evolves daily — so does my skillset.
